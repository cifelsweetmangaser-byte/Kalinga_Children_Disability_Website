Privacy Statement

Clear information about how this educational advocacy website handles information.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Our privacy commitment

Kalinga Children & Inclusion Advocacy is a school-project website that promotes inclusion and support for children with disabilities and their families in the Philippines. We aim to be clear about how this demonstration website works.

What information do we collect?

This static demonstration website does not have a database or a connected form-processing service. When you enter your name, email address, or message on the Contact Us page, the information is not sent to us or saved by this website. JavaScript only displays a confirmation message in your browser.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not intentionally use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics tools. The external services used to load photographs, fonts, and icons may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests those resources; their own privacy policies apply.

Third-party resources

Some photographs, fonts, and icons are loaded from external providers. Those providers operate independently and may process technical data when their resources are loaded. An internet connection is required to display those resources.

Changes to this statement

If the project later adds a real email service, database, analytics, or other features, this statement should be updated to explain what data is collected, why it is needed, and how it is handled.

School-project notice

This is a sample privacy statement for an educational demonstration, not legal advice. The contact form is not connected to a messaging service.

Privacy at a glance

  • No account registration
  • No intentional tracking cookies
  • No analytics installed
  • Form details are not sent or saved
